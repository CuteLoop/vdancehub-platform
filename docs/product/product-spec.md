# Product specification: thin six-pillar alpha

## Purpose

VDanceHub turns dance conversation into durable, attributable learning resources and connections.
It serves adults who lack local instruction, seek thoughtful video feedback, want to find mentors,
or want to build public dance knowledge collaboratively.

## Product principles

1. Directory and search first; recent activity is secondary.
2. Discussion, knowledge, projects, mentoring, and communities share one identity and taxonomy.
3. Community feedback stays free; professional services remain instructor-controlled.
4. Attribution, consent, moderation, privacy, and honest verification language are launch features.
5. External videos are references, not content owned or hosted by VDanceHub.

## Alpha audience and identity

- Global English-language beta for adults 18 and over.
- Members may use pseudonymous display names.
- Instructors may use professional names, but the alpha does not verify identity, qualifications,
  transactions, bookings, or outcomes.
- Location discovery stores city, region, and country only. Member home addresses are forbidden.

## Six connected activities

| Activity | Thin-alpha capability |
| --- | --- |
| Discuss | Typed questions, discussions, feedback threads, and shared resources |
| Get Feedback | Consent-gated YouTube/Instagram references and timestamped constructive replies |
| Explore | Searchable public guides, references, discussions, and projects |
| Find Mentors | Instructor profiles, offers, remote filters, and reviewed external links |
| Collaborate | Projects and guides edited by owners and named contributors, with revisions |
| Find Community | City/region scene pages and community-class proposals |

## Defining acceptance journey

An adult dancer selects a local community, creates an unlisted feedback request with a supported
external video, and confirms consent for all identifiable adults. Another member gives timestamped
feedback. The dancer discovers a relevant instructor and follows a clearly labeled, approved
external booking link. The owner then explicitly makes the reference public and a named contributor
links the discussion into a versioned public guide or project. A third dancer finds that guide using
typed search and filters rather than a feed.

## Alpha acceptance checklist

- [ ] Registration, pseudonymous profiles, reporting, blocking, and moderation
- [ ] Typed questions, feedback, resources, and discussions
- [ ] Search, tags, bookmarks, activity filters, and recent activity
- [ ] Timestamped video feedback with consent and constructive-comment rules
- [ ] Instructor profiles and mentoring offers
- [ ] Moderator-reviewed external scheduling/payment links
- [ ] Public projects with contributors, references, discussions, and milestones
- [ ] Editable guides with immutable revision history
- [ ] Safe linking among discussions, projects, knowledge, and public media references
- [ ] Approximate local-scene discovery
- [ ] Remote-friendly instructor filtering
- [ ] Public community-class proposals with reviewed external funding links

## Out of scope

Native video upload, private video storage, DMs, Stripe Connect, payment custody, verified booking,
identity or instructor verification, automated recommendations, reputation scoring, federation,
computer vision, metadata scraping, and mobile-native clients.

## Initial measures

Measure completed feedback journeys, median time to first constructive reply, guide/project reuse of
public discussions, search-to-useful-content success, report response time, and approved mentor-link
clicks. Do not optimize raw time-on-site or infinite scrolling.
