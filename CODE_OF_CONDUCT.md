# Community code of conduct

VDanceHub contributors must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of identity,
experience, dance role, dance style, body, ability, or location. Critique technique and implementation,
not people. Do not publish private correspondence, personal information, or dance footage without
permission.

Report repository conduct concerns privately using the contact process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may
remove contributions or participation that threaten member safety. A fuller, legally reviewed community
policy is required before public product launch.
